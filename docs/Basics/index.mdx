This section explains Source 2 engine fundamentals, concepts that apply across every game rather than to a single tool.

## Contents

- [What is Source 2](./what-is-source-2.md) is the orientation: the games, what the engine provides, and what changed from Source 1. 

- [Installing the Workshop Tools](./installS2Sdk/index.mdx) gets the tools onto disk. 

- [Working on content](./working-on-content/index.mdx) is the section for making something: 
    - the [addon](./working-on-content/addons/index.mdx) custom content lives in
    - the [content/ and game/](./working-on-content/content-and-game.md) split it is built around
    - the [Directory layout](./working-on-content/directory-layout.md) of every folder in it. [Decompiling assets](./working-on-content/decompiling-assets.md) is the other direction, getting editable files back out of a shipped game.

The remaining pages are engine internals: 

- [Content mounting](./content-mounting.md) (gameinfo.gi, search paths, what an addon can and cannot override), 
- [Schemas](./schemas.md) (the type system behind entity properties and data files), 
= [FGD files](./fgd.md) (the entity definitions Hammer reads)
- [Modifying the game](./modifying-the-game.md) (what an addon can replace in the base game, and what editing shipped files costs).

The binary formats themselves are documented under [File Formats](../FileFormats/index.mdx), and the individual editors under [Editor Tools](../EngineTools/index.mdx).
